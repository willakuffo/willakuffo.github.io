I design control methods that combine the adaptability of learning with the structure and guarantees of model predictive control, for robots that must work safely together.

About

I am a full-time faculty member in the Department of Engineering at Ashesi University, where I teach mechatronics and control and lead the mechatronics programme. I hold an MSc in Mechatronics Engineering from the joint Ashesi–ETH Zürich programme and a BSc in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Ashesi.

My MSc thesis, supervised by Prof. Melanie Zeilinger and Dr. Andrea Carron at ETH Zürich's Institute for Dynamic Systems and Control, used reinforcement learning to design terminal ingredients for model predictive contouring control in autonomous racing. I am now building toward learning-enhanced, distributed MPC for cooperative multi-robot systems operating under uncertainty, communication limits and tight energy budgets.

Most of my work happens between theory and hardware: I build the platforms I test on, and I teach the same way, connecting each method to a system students can run.

Research

How can learned components augment predictive control, closing the optimality gap of finite-horizon MPC and cutting computation, without giving up stability, recursive feasibility and safety? And how do these methods extend to heterogeneous teams of ground and aerial robots that must coordinate?

  • Multi-agent robotic systems
  • Learning-based MPC
  • Distributed and cooperative MPC
  • Control barrier functions
  • Reinforcement learning for control
  • State estimation (EKF)
  • Perception-aware planning
  • Visual servoing
  • Numerical optimization
  • UGVs, UAVs and manipulators

2024

Learning-based MPC for autonomous racing

MSc thesis, IDSC, ETH Zürich

  • Used the critic of an actor-critic RL agent as the MPCC terminal cost (via acados and L4CasADi): a 20-step horizon matched a 30-step horizon without terminal cost, with faster solves.
  • Warm-started the solver with the RL policy in hard-to-guess regions such as chicanes.
  • Now extending to context-conditioned terminal value functions, including a multi-agent variant.
